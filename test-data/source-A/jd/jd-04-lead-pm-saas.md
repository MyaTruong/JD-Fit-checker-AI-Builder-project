# Lead Product Manager – B2B SaaS – WorkFlowly

**Company:** WorkFlowly (fictional) – B2B workflow automation SaaS, 3,000 business customers in Southeast Asia.
**Location:** Singapore or Ho Chi Minh City
**Reports to:** VP of Product

## About the role
You will lead the Core Platform product area, manage a team of 3 Product Managers, and own the product strategy and revenue targets for this area.

## What you will do
- Set the 12–24 month product vision and strategy for the Core Platform
- Lead, coach and hire Product Managers (team of 3)
- Own the P&L and revenue targets for your product area
- Present strategy and results to the executive team and board
- Work with Sales and Customer Success on enterprise deals

## Must-have requirements
1. 7+ years of product management experience, including 2+ years leading PMs
2. Proven record of launching B2B SaaS products at scale
3. Experience owning P&L or revenue targets
4. Strong product strategy and business case skills
5. Experience working with enterprise customers and sales teams
6. Data-driven: comfortable with SQL, dashboards and experimentation
7. Excellent English communication with C-level stakeholders

## Nice-to-have
- Experience with pricing and packaging
- MBA or business background
