**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

- Own services end to end: design, code, test, deploy, monitor

1. 4+ years of backend development experience
2. Strong programming skills in Go or Java
3. Experience designing microservices and REST/gRPC APIs
4. Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL and query optimization
5. Experience with message queues such as Kafka or RabbitMQ
6. Experience with Docker, Kubernetes and AWS
7. Good understanding of system design and distributed systems
8. Computer Science degree or equivalent

## Nice-to-have
- Experience in logistics or e-commerce
- Experience with observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana)
